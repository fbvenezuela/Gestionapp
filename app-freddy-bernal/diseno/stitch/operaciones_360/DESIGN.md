## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance, command-center aesthetic calibrated for strategic political and institutional communications management. Blending contemporary editorial rigor with operational telemetry, the interface balances high-density information architecture with uncompromising typographic polish.

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
- **Rigorous & Authoritative:** Precision-engineered layouts, disciplined metric alignment, and definitive hierarchy that communicate strategic clarity.
- **Dynamic & Responsive:** Grounded in high-visibility alert markers and functional real-time tracking that convey immediate control without visual hysteria.
- **Editorial Contemporary:** Crisp modular card surfaces, calibrated warm neutrals, and sharp geometric sans-serif type reminiscent of premium analytical dossiers.

### Visual Style
The aesthetic merges **Corporate / Modern** structure with **Editorial Minimalism**:
- **Canvas Base:** Soft warm-alabaster neutrals (`#F7F7F5`) that eradicate screen glare during extended command shifts.
- **Surfaces:** Floating pure-white panels (`#FFFFFF`) framed with whisper-light geometric borders (`#E9E8E4`) instead of heavy artificial shadows.
- **Accent Cadence:** Strategic deployment of high-energy signal orange (`#FE5A03`) strictly reserved for active states, key decisions, and critical operational pathways.

## Colors

The color palette is systematically categorized into situational command tiers, neutralizing cognitive overload while ensuring instantaneous threat and progress recognition.

### Chromatic Roles
- **Primary (`#FE5A03`):** The operational beacon. Used for top-tier calls-to-action, primary operational streams, live telemetry indicators, and high-priority states.
- **Secondary (`#182A32`):** Strategic slate-navy. Anchor tone for command headers, prominent KPIs, high-contrast tooltips, and elevated modal thresholds.
- **Tertiary (`#7F8630`):** Olive green alternative route. Denotes specialized institutional channels, secondary military/civil logistics, and field validations.
- **Neutral (`#F7F7F5`):** Alabaster canvas base that softens retinal strain while holding warm contrast against white card structures.

### Functional Status Tokens
- **Success (`#10B981`):** Cleared milestones, broadcast confirmations, verified communications.
- **Warning / Risk (`#F59E0B`):** Impending deadlines, editorial bottleneck warnings, rate-limit thresholds.
- **Danger / Block (`#EF4444`):** Security anomalies, disproven narratives, blocked broadcasts, severe latency.
- **Info (`#3B82F6`):** Meta-analysis, algorithmic context, telemetry documentation.

### Text & Contrast Rules
- **Primary Text (`#111111`):** Headlines, active values, and critical data cells exceeding 14:1 contrast on pure white.
- **Secondary Text (`#666660`):** Explanatory paragraphs, metadata labels, and timestamps.
- **Muted Text (`#8E8D88`):** Disabled values, subtle breadcrumb delimiters, and table grid headers.

## Typography

The type system is powered by **Montserrat**, backed by `Arial, sans-serif`. Its geometric proportions and structural terminals deliver an authoritative editorial cadence across mission-critical screens.

### Hierarchy & Scale Guidelines
- **Display & Headlines:** Used for global metrics, war-room dashboard titles, and analytical conclusions. Kerning is slightly tightened (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) to maintain editorial punch.
- **Titles:** Serve as module headers, table section dividers, and modal titles. Weight is locked at `600` for clear visual grounding.
- **Body:** Calibrated at `400` weight with spacious line heights to ensure fatigue-free reading of dense political intelligence, field briefings, and press dossiers.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Weighted at `500` and `600` with uppercase or tracked styling (`letter-spacing: 0.02em` to `0.04em`) to establish crisp UI tags, status labels, and role pickers.
- **Timecode / Telemetry (`code-time`):** Delegated to a monospaced structure (`Courier Prime` or system monospace) to render video cuts, broadcast timestamps, and synchronized telemetry without width-jitter.

## Layout & Spacing

The operational interface is architected around a flexible **12-column responsive grid** operating on a base **8px spatial scale** (with 4px half-steps for micro-components).

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and `2rem` (32px) global margins. Supports multi-pane mission control: persistent telemetry sidebar (col-3), main visual workspace (col-6), and real-time checklist/feed (col-3).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1.5rem` (24px) canvas margin. Sidebar transitions into an off-canvas drawer or top collapsible bar.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1rem` (16px) canvas margin. Data cards stack vertically into single-column modules with edge-to-edge horizontal scrolls for data tables.

### Density & Rhythms
- **Data Densities:** Compact component padding (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) inside interactive tables and telemetry meters maximizes above-the-fold insight.
- **Module Separation:** Strategic cards and major editorial sections preserve `space-lg` or `space-xl` gaps to ensure visual breathing room between competing narrative streams.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on crisp **tonal layering** and **low-contrast micro-outlines** rather than dramatic drop shadows. This maintains a clean, rigorous editorial document feel.

### Elevation Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F7F7F5`. The passive non-interactive backdrop.
- **Tier 1 (Cards & Panels):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px continuous border (`#E9E8E4`). Flat elevation at rest.
- **Tier 2 (Hover / Active Cards & Popovers):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.03)`. Border shifts to `#D6D4CD`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Toolbars, Timecode HUDs, Modals):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or Secondary Deep Slate (`#182A32` for media HUDs) with directional shadow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(24, 42, 50, 0.12), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(24, 42, 50, 0.06)`.

### Glass & Media Overlays
In video playback modules and live telemetry stream controls, dark frosted glass is permitted:
- `background: rgba(24, 42, 50, 0.85)`
- `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`
- `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`

## Shapes

The design system employs a calibrated **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry that balances modern executive software smoothness with structural discipline.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Micro UI & Indicators (Chips, Badges, Tooltips):** `rounded` (0.375rem / 6px) to maintain sharp definition alongside small typographic metrics.
- **Standard Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Selectors):** `rounded` (0.5rem / 8px).
- **Surface Containers (Data Cards, Modal Windows, Media Players):** `rounded-lg` (0.75rem / 12px).
- **Pill Exceptions:** Operational status indicator dots and active real-time switch pills leverage fully circular/capsule styling (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to create clear differentiation between actionable surfaces and passive status badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#FE5A03` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `font-weight: 600`, `8px` radius. Hover: `#E04E00`. Focus-visible: outline `2px solid #FE5A03`, offset `2px`.
- **Secondary / Neutral:** `#FFFFFF` background, `#111111` text, border `1px solid #E9E8E4`. Hover: `#F7F7F5` background, border `#D6D4CD`.
- **Subtle / Ghost:** Transparent background, `#666660` text. Hover: `#FFFFFF` background, `#111111` text.

### Badges & Status Chips
- Integrated with a distinct **live status dot** (6px diameter).
- **Success:** `#10B98115` tint background, `#10B981` border and dot, `#065F46` label text.
- **Warning / Alert:** `#F59E0B18` tint background, `#F59E0B` border and dot, `#92400E` label text.
- **Danger / Critical:** `#EF444418` tint background, `#EF4444` border and dot, `#991B1B` label text.
- **Operational Route:** `#FE5A0315` tint background, `#FE5A03` dot, `#9A3412` label text.

### Interactive Checklist
- Items housed within white rounded cards with `#E9E8E4` separators.
- **Checkbox:** 18px square, `4px` border radius. Unchecked: `1.5px solid #8E8D88`, white background. Checked: `#FE5A03` solid background with `#FFFFFF` checkmark vector.
- **Checked Item State:** Typography shifts from `#111111` to `#8E8D88` with a subtle strike-through and a right-aligned completion timestamp (`code-time`).

### Data Tables (Dense & High-Legibility)
- Header cells: `#F7F7F5` background, `#666660` text, `label-sm` uppercase, `10px` vertical padding, border-bottom `1px solid #E9E8E4`.
- Rows: `#FFFFFF` background with alternating hover highlight `#FAFAF8`. Row height: `44px` compact.
- Critical figures, telemetry counts, and status indicators align vertically with monospace numerals.

### Media Viewer with Timecode HUD
- Edge-to-edge preview canvas with a 16:9 ratio, framed in `#182A32`.
- Floating bottom HUD overlay: frosted dark glass (`rgba(24, 42, 50, 0.85)`), containing scrub bar in `#FE5A03`, play/pause controls, and dual timecode pill (`00:04:12 / 00:15:30`) set in `code-time`.

### Demo Role Selector
- Segmented control or dropdown wrapper styled in pure white with an `#E9E8E4` perimeter.
- Role icons accompanied by a role pill (e.g., `DIRECTOR GENERAL`, `OPERADOR SALA`, `ANALISTA REDES`) rendered in `label-sm` with active background `#182A32` and white text.

### Operational Breadcrumbs
- Horizontal chain separated by forward slashes or chevrons (`#8E8D88`).
- Root elements rendered in `#666660` (`label-md`).
- Current node emphasized in `#111111` (`label-md` bold) with an optional pulse dot indicating live connection.
